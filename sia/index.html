How the priority score works

priority = P(non‑functional) × impact × 100

The model gives each pump a probability of being non‑functional (the likelihood). The impact is a blend of four factors scored 1–10:

  • People served — more people, higher impact
  • Water availability — a dry pump is rarely worth fixing
  • Isolation — distance to the nearest working pump
  • Nearby failures — broken pumps within ~20 km

The knobs set each factor's share of impact. Turn impact off to rank on raw failure probability alone.
